<objective>
Build the mobile-sales-app attendance feature: a Fichaje entry that reuses the existing `mobile_scanner`
infrastructure to scan the caja's daily `/m/fichaje` QR and POST `/attendance/punch`, role-branched result
screens (vendedor entrada/salida with worked hours; revendedor store-authorized), and the clock-in home gate
(when `/mobile/me` reports `clockedIn:false`, the vendedor's Catálogo/scanner/sales are disabled until they
clock in). Ends with a blocking human-verify UAT.

Purpose: The vendedor Flutter app (Waves 3-4) gains an "출퇴근 등록" entry point. Single QR, role-routed by the
backend (37-06). Before clocking in, the app steers the vendedor to fichaje only; the backend
RequireAttendanceGuard is the authority, the app mirrors it for UX (no round-trips per action).

Output: attendance data/provider/repository, fichaje scanner sheet (deeplink parse reusing the Wave 4 pattern),
role-branched result screen, home clock-in gate, revendedor store-auth prompt, a deeplink parse unit test, and
a UAT checkpoint.

SCOPE (authoritative):
- vendedor fichaje (entrada/salida) is the LIVE part of this wave (spec L169).
- revendedor store-auth prompt UI is built here but its catalog is gated on Phase 24 — this wave wires the
  punch → store_authorized flow + the "not authorized" prompt so it activates cleanly with Phase 24.
- Reuse `parseStockDeeplink` pattern (qr_scanner_sheet.dart) — add a sibling `parseFichajeDeeplink`. Do NOT
  reinvent the scanner; MobileScanner + viewfinder is already proven.
</objective>
