<objective>
Implement the RestaurantDelivery service (order intake, state transitions, Entregado→PAID settlement, cancel), DTOs, controller, and module wiring. This is the control core: REQ-5 (intake), REQ-6 (board transitions + rider guard), REQ-7 (efectivo→por_cobrar gap), REQ-8 (qr auto-close linkage), plus REQ-2/REQ-4 (Sale 1:1 + source='delivery').

Purpose: This service enforces the phase's central invariant — *Entregado ≠ order closed*. Cash orders stay in `por_cobrar` after delivery (no box movement here; that happens in rider settlement, plan 06). QR closes via the EXISTING intent-centric mp-webhook (plan 06 hook). The webhook resolves a payment by `external_reference → MpPaymentIntent` and acts on `intent.pendingVentaId` — it never resolves a Sale by saleId. Therefore the load-bearing linkage for REQ-8 is established HERE at intake: for `paymentMode='qr'`, the MpPaymentIntent must carry `pendingVentaId = sale.id` (mirroring the mesa QR precedent), and since `RestaurantDelivery.saleId === sale.id`, plan 06 can look up the delivery via `intent.pendingVentaId`. This service reuses RestaurantSaleService's single-TX sale↔meta pattern and MpQrService's intent creation; it does NOT reimplement money/stock/comanda/MP logic.
Output: complete delivery service + controller + module registered in app.module.ts.
</objective>
