<objective>
Add the two automated-collection paths: (REQ-9) the delivery-app L1 payout CSV reconciliation service (MinIO store + fixed-template parse + externalRef exact-match) and (REQ-8) the MercadoPago QR auto-close hook that closes a `paymentMode=qr` delivery when its EXISTING payment-intent webhook confirms payment.

Purpose: These are the two non-cash collection terminals. QR closes automatically via the EXISTING intent-centric mp-webhook (no new webhook infra — reuse). The webhook resolves an `MpPaymentIntent` by `external_reference`, runs idempotency on the intent, calls `wallets.creditOnSale(intent, ...)`, and (post-commit) emits via `intent.pendingVentaId`. There is NO saleId-keyed sale resolution in the webhook — the load-bearing linkage is `intent.pendingVentaId === sale.id` (set at intake in plan 04, mirroring the mesa QR precedent). App orders sit in `conciliacion` until a payout CSV matches them by `externalRef` (D-08, exact match only, no tolerance — out of scope). Both must keep QR/app orders out of cash `por_cobrar`.
Output: CSV service + DTO + controller endpoint + module wiring (MinioModule); minimal additive hook in mercadopago/webhook/mp-webhook.service.ts (+ RestaurantDeliveryModule import in MercadopagoModule).
</objective>
